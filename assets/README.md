# Illustration and Font Credits

Fruit illustrations are decorative OpenAI image-generation outputs, created for this project on 15 October 2026. They are not observations, maps or evidence. Transparent alpha is retained in the compressed WebP files.

Built-in image-generation tool, bowl prompt: compact shallow pale pink ceramic bowl filled with apples, pears, oranges, banana, grapes, strawberries, blueberries and green leaves; soft hand-painted gouache; crisp silhouettes; subtle texture; blush, mint, butter-yellow and berry palette; isolated whole bowl; no scene, text, faces or logos; transparent background.

Built-in image-generation tool, icon prompt: eight matching fruit illustrations in a four-by-two asset sheet, apple, pear, banana, orange, purple grapes, strawberry, blueberries and mango; soft colourful gouache, crisp silhouettes, subtle texture, generous transparent separation, no faces, text, external shadows or background. The sheet was separated into eight compressed assets in `icons/` without changing their proportions or alpha.

Saved assets: `fruit-bowl.webp` and `icons/apple.webp`, `pear.webp`, `banana.webp`, `orange.webp`, `grapes.webp`, `strawberry.webp`, `blueberries.webp`, `mango.webp`.

Fraunces and Nunito Sans were obtained from Google Fonts and are self-hosted in `fonts/`. Their SIL Open Font License files are included alongside the font files. No font service request is needed by the published page.
