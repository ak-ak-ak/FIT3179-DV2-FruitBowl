Where Your Fruit Bowl Comes From

Where Australia grows its fruit, how production is changing, and what reaches overseas markets.

What's in the bowl?

Australia grows a wide variety of fruit, but production is concentrated in particular regions. Follow where it grows, how production is changing, and where some of it goes overseas.

Where it grows

Different fruits come from different parts of Australia. Compare the states first, then look more closely at the growing regions of bananas and avocados.

How it has changed

Fruit production has changed unevenly. Some crops have grown substantially, while others have declined. Production volume tells one part of the story; the value of that fruit tells another.

Beyond our borders

Australia exports fruit to markets across Asia, the Pacific and beyond. Different fruits have different overseas destinations, so this final map compares five export categories.

Australia's fruit production is varied, but individual crops depend on particular growing regions. Changes in production and value differ by fruit, and overseas markets add another part to the story.
